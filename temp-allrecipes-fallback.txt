Directions
----------

1.   Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (180 degrees C) and line a baking sheet with parchment or foil. If using foil, grease foil lightly.

 
2.   Separate dough into 8 biscuits, and place on the prepared sheet pan. Gently tease apart half of each biscuit’s layers, almost to the opposite edge of the biscuit, so it opens up like a book.

 
3.   Fold 2 slices of thinly sliced deli ham to fit each biscuit, and place on the bottom half of each biscuit. Top with 1/2 slice of cheese, folded to fit the biscuits. Gently fold top halves of biscuit dough over cheese.

 
4.   Brush each biscuit with melted butter and sprinkle with everything seasoning.

 
5.   Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown, 15 to 20 minutes. Cool about 5 minutes and serve warm.

These were tasty. I love a savory breakfast and that Everything Bagel seasoning brings the party. While the flavor was nice this dish had several issues. The ham would be better cut into little bits as it tried to pull out in one piece when eating. I used frozen Grands biscuits and tried to split them in half while still frozen which didn't work and I ended up with biscuit chips. I thawed them out a little first and that worked better, but that dough was soft and easily misshapen. I formed it back into round biscuits as best as I could and proceeded. This was easy to do overall and would be even easier with a homemade roll out and cut biscuit rather than a premade one. It didn't say to pinch or try to seal the edges shut. I think I'd prefer sausage or bacon to ham so I might try this again with a different meat. For kids, I'd probably leave that bagel seasoning off or just use sesame seeds or poppy seeds. As a picky eater if this had ended up on my plate, I would have gone hungry as the onion from the seasoning is prominent and it leaves a long lasting aftertaste of onion. Maybe garlic too. Those processed biscuits left a unpleasant greasy film in my mouth after eating. If I were to make this again, I'd make my own biscuits and cut the ham into small bits like a ham and cheese hot pocket. 3.5 stars from me as I liked it, but it needs improvement. Edited

These were tasty. I love a savory breakfast and that Everything Bagel seasoning brings the party. While the flavor was nice this dish had several issues. The ham would be better cut into little bits as it tried to pull out in one piece when eating. I used frozen Grands biscuits and tried to split them in half while still frozen which didn't work and I ended up with biscuit chips. I thawed them out a little first and that worked better, but that dough was soft and easily misshapen. I formed it back into round biscuits as best as I could and proceeded. This was easy to do overall and would be even easier with a homemade roll out and cut biscuit rather than a premade one. It didn't say to pinch or try to seal the edges shut. I think I'd prefer sausage or bacon to ham so I might try this again with a different meat. For kids, I'd probably leave that bagel seasoning off or just use sesame seeds or poppy seeds. As a picky eater if this had ended up on my plate, I would have gone hungry as the onion from the seasoning is prominent and it leaves a long lasting aftertaste of onion. Maybe garlic too. Those processed biscuits left a unpleasant greasy film in my mouth after eating. If I were to make this again, I'd make my own biscuits and cut the ham into small bits like a ham and cheese hot pocket. 3.5 stars from me as I liked it, but it needs improvement.

1.   1
